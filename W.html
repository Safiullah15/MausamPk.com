Weather tips for Pakistan

Staying safe in a heatwave

Drink water often, avoid going out between noon and 4 PM, wear light cotton clothes and check on elderly neighbours. Headache, dizziness and very hot dry skin are warning signs that need medical help.

Getting ready for monsoon

Clear drains near your home, keep a torch and power bank ready, avoid low-lying roads during heavy rain and never touch fallen electric wires. Check the 7-day forecast before travelling to the north.

Winter fog on motorways

Dense fog often closes parts of the motorway in December and January. Use low-beam headlights, keep a safe gap and check the hourly forecast, since fog is usually thickest from midnight to early morning.

Visiting the northern areas

Weather in Murree, Swat, Gilgit and Skardu can change within hours. Carry warm layers even in summer, and look at the rain chance and temperature range before you set out.
